Common questions

How is the logo applied?

Blind debossing, engraving, foil stamping or a metal plate — which one works best depends on the material. Send us the file and we show your logo on the chosen material before anything goes into production. Logo setup is $20 per order.

What materials can I choose from?

Genuine leather, faux leather, wood, hardcover and fabric, plus acrylic on some menu formats. Every piece is handcrafted and checked before it ships.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum for stock items, and you can order a single piece as a sample. Branded runs have a minimum that depends on the material — ask in the form and we’ll confirm it with the quote.

How long does it take?

In-stock items arrive in 1–4 business days; anything ordered before 1 PM (PST in the US, CET in the EU) ships the next day. Branded production runs longer and the quote states the exact date.

Can I see a sample before ordering the full run?

Yes. We produce a single branded piece first so you can check the emboss, the colour and the feel in the room before committing to the volume.
