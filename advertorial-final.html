Before you ask

Why order the categories together?

Because the effect comes from repetition. Menu covers bought in spring from one supplier and notebooks bought in autumn from another will not match, and two good browns that differ read worse than one honest black.

What can actually be personalised?

Debossing in Blind, Gold, Copper, Black or Silver; engraving in six colours; leather, faux leather, wood, fabric, hardcover and acrylic; paper weight 130/200/300 gsm and 1 to 12 sheets. Some pieces carry up to 250 configurations.
