Why order the categories together?

Because the effect comes from repetition. Menu covers bought in spring from one supplier and presenters bought in autumn from another will not match, and two good browns that differ read worse than one honest black. Ordering together also puts the whole run into one volume band, which is where the price drops.

Materials and sizes

Genuine leather, faux leather, wood, veneered HDF, hardcover, fabric and acrylic. Menu formats cover A4, A5, A3 folded, US Letter and Legal; wine and drinks formats are narrower. The Capri leather alone comes in twenty colours.

How the logo is applied

Blind debossing, engraving, foil stamping or a metal plate — the material decides which reads best. Setup is $20 per order, charged once however many pieces are in the run, and re-orders reuse the same tooling.

Lead times and shipping

Stock lines: 1–4 business days, shipped from the US or EU warehouse, next-day dispatch on orders placed before 1 PM PST / CET. Branded runs take longer and the quote states the exact date rather than a range.
